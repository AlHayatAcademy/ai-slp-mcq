- First visit opens directly on a question, with no setup form.

- Wrong choice is distinguished from the correct choice; a correct selection is also acknowledged.
- Other answer options become locked for that round.
- Next remains disabled before selection and is enabled immediately afterward.
- Next navigation and current position survive a reload.
- Incorrect answers appear in Review mistakes.
- Reattempting an incorrect item correctly clears it from the current mistake list.
- One-question round completion reports 1/1 correctly; no retry of missed questions is offered when none were missed.
- Quick revision search finds prompt-dependence concepts with answers and explanations.
- Phone (390px), tablet (768px) and desktop layouts inspected.
- On the phone, selecting an answer scrolls to its explanation; Previous/Next remain in a fixed bottom bar.

## Limits

These are application and consistency checks, not psychometric validation or a guarantee of the actual exam's contents. Coverage is substantive and section-wide, not one question per source sentence. Clinical figures remain source-bound. Offline caching is implemented but a complete network-disconnection test was not run.
